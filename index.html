●  Who we are

A community-driven club empowering youth to bridge impactful social action with modern fellowship.

Sponsored by the Rotary Club of Mumbai Western Elite under Rotary International District 3141, we are students and young professionals from Goregaon and beyond who trade late mornings for early mornings — drives, drills, runs and rallies across Mumbai's western suburbs since June 2016.
