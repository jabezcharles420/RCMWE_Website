## Community Impact & Social Welfare

### Mega Aarey Youth & Child Welfare Drive (11th July 2026)

**Brief:** A multi-module community empowerment outreach conducted across Aarey Milk Colony covering youth education, physical and digital safety, and direct nutritional relief.

**Impact:** Educated 90+ students (Grades 3–11) on academic discipline, first-aid response, emergency hotlines, and digital fraud prevention; distributed 112 essential stationery kits; and served 99 hot, multi-course nutritious meal boxes in collaboration with The Altruist Hotels.

### Freedom Bins – Civic Waste Management (15th August 2026)

**Brief:** An Independence Day cleanliness campaign promoting the Swachh Bharat Mission around Oberoi Mall and Goregaon East.

**Impact:** Distributed 100+ branded, hook-fitted dustbins directly to auto-rickshaw drivers to curb on-road passenger littering and promote clean urban transport.

### Baaghbaan – Senior Citizen Outreach (8th July 2026)

**Brief:** An intergenerational fellowship visit to the elderly residents of Assisi Bhavan, Aarey Road.

**Impact:** Brought music, companionship, recreational games, and nutrition gift packs to 30+ senior citizens.

### Empowerment for Specially Abled Individuals (14th July 2026)

**Brief:** An inclusive recreational and life-skills camp hosted at Shashi Mangalam School.

**Impact:** Engaged 50+ specially abled students through adapted cricket matches, dance therapy, digital screen-time guidance, and body boundary safety workshops.

### Grassroots Digital ID & Legal Rights Assistance (13th & 16th July 2026)

**Brief:** Ground-level legal and digital empowerment drives for transit drivers and domestic workers.

**Impact:** Educated 35 local drivers on legal rights during road stops, and onboarded 25 grassroots workers onto DigiLocker to permanently secure their government identification documents.

## Environmental Conservation & Climate Action

### Mega Plantation Drive 2.0 (12th July 2026)

**Brief:** A mass afforestation drive organized near Aarey Dairy in collaboration with Rotary to counter urban heatwaves.

**Impact:** Planted 100+ native saplings to restore and expand green cover across Mumbai's ecological hub.

### Saviorthon 10K – Run for Nature (23rd August 2026)

**Brief:** A 10 km charity marathon held at Bandra Fort in association with 4Memories Event and Saksham Social Trust.

**Impact:** Promoted ecological conservation and healthy living while channelling dedicated runner registration funds toward women's empowerment initiatives.

## Entrepreneurship, Fundraising & Public Reach

### Muskaan Exhibition Stall (4th July 2026)

**Brief:** A 12-hour interactive entertainment and merchandise stall managed during a Rotary-led exhibition at NESCO, Goregaon.

**Impact:** Engaged attendees from an 800+ footfall with games, customized accessories, and instant souvenir portraits, raising a net profit of ₹11,000 for club welfare projects.

### Promotion of Seva Mahotsav (15th July 2026)

**Brief:** A targeted social media campaign amplifying district service initiatives.

**Impact:** Partnered with digital creator Yashvi (@yashvii_a) to broadcast the message of social service across a digital audience of 50,000+ followers.

## Youth Fellowship, Sports & Wellness

### The Game Night Tournament & House Championship Launch (9th August 2026)

**Brief:** An interactive four-house tournament kicking off the club's year-long internal fellowship league.

**Impact:** Engaged 18 Board and General Body members through team-building games, establishing strong club culture and active member retention.

### Turf Wars (19th July 2026)

**Brief:** A multi-sport morning featuring back-to-back football and cricket matches at Nidhivan Turf.

**Impact:** Brought together 24 members across randomized teams to promote physical fitness and cross-board camaraderie.

### Pranic Healing & Wellness Session (3rd July 2026)

**Brief:** A drug-free holistic wellness workshop held at Tulip Hall, DB Woods.

**Impact:** Provided 18 participants with guided breathing techniques, stress-relief exercises, and mental health awareness.
