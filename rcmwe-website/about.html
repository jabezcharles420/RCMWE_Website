●  About the club

Ten years of early mornings.

Chartered in June 2016 under Rotary International District 3141, the Rotaract Club of Mumbai Western Elite is a community-driven NGO of young people who bridge impactful social action with modern fellowship, wellness and professional networking.

2016 chartered → today

Mission

Empowering youth to bridge impactful social action with modern fellowship, wellness and professional networking.

Theme 2026–27

"Be the Dawn. Make the Impact."

This year's rallying call: show up before sunrise, leave every venue brighter than we found it.

●  Milestones

The road here.

June 2016

Chartered

The club receives its charter — Club ID 213639 — sponsored by the Rotary Club of Mumbai Western Elite, joining Rotary International District 3141.

2016 — 2025

A decade in the western suburbs

Weekly meetings, community drives and district events across Goregaon, Aarey and beyond build the club's culture of showing up — rain, exams or deadlines notwithstanding.

June 2026

"Dawn of Impact" begins

A new Rotaract year opens under the theme "Be the Dawn. Make the Impact." — with a refreshed board, four avenues of service and a packed July calendar.

July — August 2026

Sixty days, twelve projects

The club's largest documented sprint yet: welfare outreach in Aarey, civic cleanliness for Independence Day, afforestation, legal-aid camps, fundraising stalls and a house-league launch — all on record, all quantified.
